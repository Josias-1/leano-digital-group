# Leano brand-asset library

Every asset is derived from the approved source lockup, [`public/images/leano-digital-group-logo.svg`](../images/leano-digital-group-logo.svg). Do not redraw or alter the mark.

## Website and app icons

- `web/leano-mark-16x16.png`, `32x32.png`, and `48x48.png` — browser/favicon fallbacks.
- `web/leano-mark-180x180.png` — Apple touch icon.
- `web/leano-mark-192x192.png`, `512x512.png`, and `1024x1024.png` — PWA, app stores, and product use.
- `app/icon.png` and `app/apple-icon.png` are already connected to the Next.js site. `app/manifest.ts` supplies installable-app metadata.

## Merchandise and product

- `merch/t-shirt-chest-lockup.svg`, `cap-front-mark.svg`, `notebook-cover.svg`, and `sticker-mark.svg` — scalable artwork for suppliers.
- `product/portal-header.svg` and the app-icon PNGs — dashboards, client portals, and product surfaces.

SVG files are vector artwork, so they can scale cleanly for print and signage. Share the original SVG with a professional printer, who can convert it to the printer’s required CMYK/PDF workflow.
